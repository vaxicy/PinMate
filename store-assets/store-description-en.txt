PinMate is an AI assistant for Pinterest creators: upload a Pin image and it writes the title, description, topic tags and alt text — filled into the publish form in one click.

• Written from the image, not a template
• Product links in one click — optional affiliate disclosure, and a Replace / Add choice when a tag already exists
• Optionally switches on "Publish at a later date" for every Pin
• Cloud or local models — with a local model your images never leave your machine
• English & Chinese UI, built-in guide; keys and settings stay in your browser

Add your own API key (or pick a local model) in Settings to start.

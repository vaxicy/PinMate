PinMate is an AI assistant for Pinterest creators: upload a Pin image and it reads the picture, then writes a matching title, description, topic tags and alt text — all filled into the publish form in one click.

• Written from the image itself — not a template
• One-click fill — title, description, tags and alt text go straight into Pinterest
• Product links: optional auto-fill once pasted, with the affiliate disclosure ticked for you — and it stays off once you switch it off yourself
• Publish later: optionally turns on Pinterest's "Publish at a later date" for every Pin
• Works with cloud and local models — with a local model your images never leave your machine, and images are downscaled so local generation stays fast
• English & Chinese UI; keys and settings stay in your browser
• Built-in guide covering setup and common issues

Add your own API key (or pick a local model) in Settings to start.

=== English Summary ===

AI writes Pinterest titles, descriptions, tags & alt text for SEO and fills them in with one click — product links included.

=== English Detailed Description ===

PinMate is an AI assistant for Pinterest creators: upload a Pin image and it reads the picture, then writes a matching title, description, topic tags and alt text — all filled into the publish form in one click.

• Written from the image itself — not a template
• One-click fill; copy or regenerate anytime
• Product links and product tags too
• English & Chinese, keys kept in your browser

Add your own API key in Settings to start.
